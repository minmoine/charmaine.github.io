Our Solar System

Eight Worlds,
One Star

Our solar system is home to eight remarkable planets, each with its own story written in rock, gas, and ice. The rocky inner worlds — Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars — forged close to the sun's heat. Beyond the asteroid belt, the gas giants Jupiter and Saturn reign supreme, their gravity shaping the fate of everything around them.

Further still, the ice giants Uranus and Neptune drift in the cold outer dark, ringed by moons that harbour secrets we are only beginning to understand. Every planet is a world unto itself — waiting to be explored.
