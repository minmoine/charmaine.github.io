Our Solar System

Eight Worlds,
One Star

There are eight planets in our solar system that have all fascinating stories all written on rock, gas, and ice. Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars are the rocky inner worlds that were formed close to the heat of the sun. Outside the asteroid belt, the gas giants Jupiter and Saturn dominate with gravity ruling over all that is in their orbit.

Even farther away, the frigid outer worlds of Uranus and Neptune wander through the night, with their moons holding secrets to be unlocked. Each planet is a world in itself – waiting to be discovered.
