Mercury

The smallest planet in our solar system and the closest to the Sun — a scorched world of extreme temperatures and ancient craters.

Facts About Mercury

Distance from Sun 57.9M km

Mercury orbits closer to the Sun than any other planet, completing a full orbit every 88 Earth days.

Surface Temperature −180° to 430°C

Without an atmosphere to retain heat, temperatures swing more than 600 degrees between night and day.

Moons 0

Mercury has no moons — its weak gravity and proximity to the Sun make capturing satellites nearly impossible.

Diameter 4,879 km

The smallest planet in the solar system — only slightly larger than Earth's Moon.

Day Length 59 Earth Days

A single day on Mercury lasts longer than its entire year, rotating extremely slowly on its axis.

Gravity 3.7 m/s²

Mercury's gravity is just 38% of Earth's — you would weigh less than half as much standing on its surface.
